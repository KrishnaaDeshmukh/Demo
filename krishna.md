🕉️ Krishna

Lord Krishna is one of the most loved and important deities in Hinduism. He is considered the 8th avatar of Vishnu, the protector of the universe.

📖 Birth and Early Life

Born in Mathura, in present-day Mathura

Parents: Devaki and Vasudeva

Raised in Gokul and Vrindavan

He was born in prison because his uncle Kansa feared a prophecy that Krishna would defeat him.
🎵 Childhood Stories

Krishna is famous for:

Stealing butter (Makhan Chor 😄)

Playing flute

Lifting Govardhan Hill

His love and devotion story with Radha

⚔️ Role in Mahabharata

Krishna played a very important role in the epic Mahabharata.

He was the guide and charioteer of Arjuna in the Kurukshetra war.
